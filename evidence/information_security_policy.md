# Information Security Policy — Dreams Home Estate Stay
Document owner: Manager
Approved by: Owner
Date: 2026-10-15
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
This policy sets out how Dreams Home Estate Stay protects guest personal data,
booking records, and payment information.

## Scope
Applies to all staff, the front-desk PC, the booking/property-management system,
Wi-Fi, CCTV, and any device accessing guest data.

## Policy statements
- All systems holding guest data must use multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- Access to guest data is granted on a least-privilege basis.
- Guest ID scans and payment references must be stored securely and encrypted.
- Security incidents must be reported to the Manager within 24 hours.
- This policy is reviewed at least annually.

## Roles and responsibilities
The Manager is accountable for information security. All staff are responsible
for following this policy and completing security awareness training.
